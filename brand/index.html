01 — Foundation

What Phoxta stands for

Building a business used to take a year and a team. Phoxta collapses that to a choice: pick a validated, AI-powered business, make it your own, and go live in days. Our brand has to feel as effortless and capable as the product.

Mission

Put ownership of a working, AI-run business within everyone's reach.

Vision

A world where starting a business is as simple as choosing one.

Positioning statement

For aspiring owners and operators, Phoxta is the marketplace of validated, AI-powered businesses you can own and run from day one. Unlike build-it-yourself platforms or business brokers, every Phoxta business ships fully operational, with one AI agent running sales and support across every channel — and you own it outright.

02 — Verbal identity

How Phoxta sounds

We write the way a sharp, generous operator talks: plain, confident and concrete. We lead with the outcome, cut the hype, and respect the reader's time.

Primary tagline

Own a business that already works.

Supporting line — Launch niche businesses powered entirely by automated AI pipelines.

Messaging pillars

Own it

Yours outright — brand, domain, payments, customers.

Live in days

A working storefront and app, ready to sell.

AI runs it

One agent across phone, chat, SMS and email, 24/7.

Validated

Proven designs you can preview before you buy.

✓ Voice — do
  • Lead with the outcome ("go live in days").
  • Short sentences. Plain words. Active voice.
  • Be specific and concrete — real numbers, real steps.
  • Sound confident and human, never robotic.
  • Use sentence case for most UI and headlines.
✕ Voice — don't
  • No hype, buzzwords or empty superlatives.
  • No fake scarcity or "free trial" gimmicks.
  • Don't bury the value in jargon or acronyms.
  • Don't over-promise — Phoxta is honest by design.
  • Avoid ALL CAPS for full sentences.
