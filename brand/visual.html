Usage ratio — 60 / 30 / 10

60% neutral light · 30% ink/dark · 10% Vermilion. Vermilion is a spice, not a base — never flood a layout with it.

Accessibility

Maintain WCAG AA (4.5:1 body / 3:1 large). Vermilion #F0460E on white passes for large text/icons; for body text on light, use Ink. White on Vermilion is AA for large only — prefer it on buttons/labels, not paragraphs.

05 — Typography

Typography

One typeface, used with discipline: DM Sans. Geometric, friendly and highly legible at every size — from a 92px hero to 12px labels. Tighten tracking on large display; keep body comfortable.

Rules

07 — Imagery

Photography & illustration

Real people, real product, warm natural light. We show owners in their element and the product on screen — candid, optimistic, premium. Frame everything in soft rounded corners; use a Vermilion duotone for hero moments.

Above: natural product/people photography, plus a Vermilion duotone treatment (last) for hero and campaign art.

✓ Imagery — do
  • Candid, human moments with natural light.
  • Show the real product UI and storefronts.
  • Rounded corners (16–24px), generous negative space.
  • Apply Vermilion duotone for bold, branded heroes.
✕ Imagery — don't
  • No stiff, staged or cliché stock photography.
  • Don't mix many filters or heavy gradients.
  • Don't crop faces awkwardly or over-busy the frame.
  • Don't place light text on a light part of a photo — add a scrim.
