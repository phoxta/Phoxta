08 — UI & components

Interface system

The product and marketing share one component language: pill buttons with a signature double-arrow, rounded-20 cards, bracket eyebrows, and quiet, reveal-on-scroll motion.

Buttons

Primary = Ink. Brand = Vermilion (reserve for the single key action). Signature hover swaps the label and slides a double arrow.

10 — Governance

Keeping it consistent

A brand is only as strong as its consistency. When in doubt, choose the simpler, quieter option — and ask.

✓ Always
  • Use approved logo files and the Vermilion + neutral palette.
  • Set everything in DM Sans.
  • Lead with the outcome; keep copy plain and honest.
  • Give the logo and content room to breathe.
✕ Never
  • Recolour, distort, rotate or add effects to the logo.
  • Flood layouts with Vermilion or invent new brand colours.
  • Use hype, fake scarcity or "free trial" language.
  • Mix in other typefaces or heavy photo filters.

Questions, exceptions or new asset requests → hello@phoxta.com. This guide is living; propose changes rather than working around it.
